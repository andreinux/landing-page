COLLEGE OF TECHNOLOGY

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

The Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) program is designed to transform students into versatile tech professionals capable of navigating the ever-evolving digital landscape. This program focuses on the installation, operation, development, and administration of computer systems and network infrastructures to solve real-world business problems.

Major Competencies

Logic, Algorithms,
and Full-Stack Development.

Network Protocols, Server Management,
and Cloud Infrastructure.

SQL/NoSQL, Data Modeling,
and Information Storage.

Ethical Hacking, Risk Management,
and Data Encryption.
